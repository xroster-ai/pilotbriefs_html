✈ Download & set up

Three minutes
from download to first answer.

Always the latest signed build. The app is free to download — a licence key, or a free 7-day trial, unlocks it. One key activates one device, on either platform.

Download for Mac (.dmg) Download for Windows (.exe) Start a free 7-day trial
1

Download and open the .dmg

Click Download for Mac above. When it finishes, open the downloaded PilotBrief.dmg from your Downloads folder.

2

Drag PilotBrief into Applications

In the window that opens, drag the PilotBrief icon onto the Applications folder, then open it from Applications or Launchpad.

3

Open it — and the one keychain prompt

Double-click PilotBrief. It is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens normally — no "damaged" message, no right-click, no Terminal.

On first launch macOS may ask to allow PilotBrief to use its own PilotBrief Safe Storage keychain item, where it keeps your settings encrypted. Click Always Allow and enter your Mac password once.

That prompt is the app unlocking its own storage, nothing else, and it appears only because the build is freshly signed.
1

Download and run the installer

Click Download for Windows above, then run the downloaded PilotBrief-Setup.exe from your Downloads folder.

2

Pass SmartScreen

Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app is new and not yet widely downloaded. This is expected.

Click More info, then Run anyway to continue the install.

If your browser flags the download, choose Keep or Keep anyway.
✓

Activate your licence

Open PilotBrief and go to Settings → Activate licence. Enter the email you bought with and one licence key — from your purchase email, the page shown after checkout, or your trial confirmation — in the form PB-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX.

Each key activates one device. Open the app on a new machine with the same key and the old device is released automatically.

Then load your manuals. To run with no internet and no API key, open "Use PilotBrief without internet" in Settings and let it set up the on-device model.
