Download & set up

Get PilotBrief on your Mac or PC ✈️

Download, install, and activate in about three minutes. This guide covers the one-time security prompt on Mac and Windows, and how to enter your licence key.

Download for Mac (.dmg) Download for Windows (.exe)
Always the latest version — free to download; a licence key (or free trial) unlocks it. Your key works on both platforms (one device per key).
1

1 · Download & open the .dmg

Click Download for Mac above. When it finishes, open the downloaded PilotBrief.dmg from your Downloads.

2

2 · Drag PilotBrief into Applications

In the window that opens, drag the PilotBrief icon onto the Applications folder. Then open PilotBrief from Applications (or Launchpad).

3

3 · Open it — and the one keychain prompt

Just double-click PilotBrief. It's signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens normally — no "damaged" message, no right-click, no Terminal.

The first time it opens, macOS may ask to allow PilotBrief to use its own "PilotBrief Safe Storage" item in your keychain (that's where it keeps your settings encrypted). Click Always Allow and enter your Mac password once — you won't be asked again.

This is the app unlocking its own storage, nothing else — and it only appears because this version is freshly signed.
1

1 · Download & run the installer

Click Download for Windows above, then run the downloaded PilotBrief-Setup.exe from your Downloads.

2

2 · Bypass SmartScreen

Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app is new and not yet widely downloaded. This is expected and safe.

Click More info, then Run anyway to continue the install.

If your browser flags the download, choose Keep / Keep anyway.
✓

Activate your licence

Open PilotBrief → Settings → Activate licence. Enter the email you bought with and one licence key (from your purchase email or the post-checkout page, formatted like PB-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX). Each key activates one device — open it on a new machine and the old one releases automatically.

That's it — ask anything from your own manuals and get grounded, page-cited answers. Want it to work with no internet and no API key? In Settings, open "Use PilotBrief without internet" and let it set up the on-device AI for you.
