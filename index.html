Conformity audit

Check the operator's manual against the regulator's checklist.

  • Add the checklist — the requirements to check against
  • Add the documents to audit — the operator's OM-A or equivalent, which is not added to your library
  • Each item is assessed in turn, and the audit runs in the background while you keep working
  • Findings come back as compliant, non-compliant or remarks, with items the app could not determine flagged for your check
  • Export the result to Word or PDF when you are done reviewing

✈ Scenario mode

The hard part is not search.
It is choosing the right checklist.

A keyword match hands you the most frightening title on the page. PilotBrief matches the legally-binding Condition statement instead, distinguishes an announced failure from an unannunciated one, and asks a Yes/No question when a detail would change the checklist. Every line it returns is your manual's own wording with the page it came from: it does not paraphrase procedures, and it does not invent them.

Is this safe to use in the flight deck?

PilotBrief is a study & reference aid. It reproduces your manuals verbatim and cites the exact page, but it is not a replacement for the official approved documentation or your operator's procedures. Always verify against your current approved manuals and apply CRM.

Where do my manuals go?

Nowhere. PilotBrief runs on your own Mac or PC and your documents never leave your computer. You bring your own AI key, and questions go directly from your machine to the provider — never through us.

Does it really work offline?

Yes. Index your manuals once, add a small free on-device model, and PilotBrief answers from your library with no internet and no API key — including scenario reasoning, briefs, and study tools.

What do I need to run it?

A Mac or Windows computer, plus a way to power the AI: your own OpenAI and/or Anthropic API key (a few dollars of usage covers heavy use), prepaid AI tokens you can buy inside the app (optional in-app purchase; terms apply), or the free on-device model for offline use.
